\section{Conclusion}

We have demonstrated that the Fe-on-MgO interface is thermodynamically predisposed to island growth mode. By implementing the active learning framework with a biased exploration strategy, we successfully sampled a representative ensemble, allowing for the categorization of the PES into distinct island and flat growth modes. Our analysis, utilizing KDE-based state density and Boltzmann probability distributions, reveals that although increasing temperature promotes the occupation of higher-energy configurations, the substantial energy barrier of approximately 0.18 eV/atom renders thermal fluctuations alone insufficient to fully suppress islanding. These findings provide a robust quantitative foundation for understanding growth-mode transitions and indicate that achieving atomically flat Fe-on-MgO requires additional external interventions to overcome the thermodynamic preference for island formation.
